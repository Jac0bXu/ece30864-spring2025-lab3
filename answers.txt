1. Agile Pros: 1. Agile is flexible and good at meeting changing project needs. 2. agile method provides quick feedback which is valuable for development. 

Agile Cons: 1. It is hard to properly document stuff while using agile development method. 2. Agile is not good at developing complex systems.

2.Scrum Master’s job is to make sure that the team adhere to the agile process and make sure the teams are collaborating with each other. They need to do all the Jira related stuff and host standups and organize sprints.

The Product Owner’s job is to define the needs of the product and representing the needs of the customers. They need to set the direction of the team and deliever stake holder interests.

3. Sprint Planning Meeting:

determine what to do in the upcoming sprint

determine story points

start the sprint.

Daily Scrum/Standup Meeting:

discuss what has people done in the previous day.

Plan what need to be done in the upcoming day.

Sprint Retrospective Meeting:

Discuss the output of the sprint.

closes the sprint.

4. Epic:

An epic captures a large body of work that can be broken down into several smaller stories

Story:

A story is a software system requirement that is expressed in a few short sentences.

Bug:

A Bug is a problem that prevent a feature from being implemented.

Task:

A Task is a task that need to be done to complete a part of a story.
