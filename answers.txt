1. Agile Pros: 1. Agile is flexible and good at meeting changing project needs. 2. agile method provides quick feedback which is valuable for development. 

Agile Cons: 1. It is hard to properly document stuff while using agile development method. 2. Agile is not good at developing complex systems.

2.Scrum Master’s job is to make sure that the team adhere to the agile process and make sure the teams are collaborating with each other.

The Product Owner’s job is to define the needs of the product and representing the needs of the customers.
